Features

Built for the email,
not the transcript.

A transcript is a means to an end. Everything in Fieldnote exists to get a correct, specific follow-up out the door.

Receipts on every line

Click any sentence in a draft to hear the exact moment it came from. If nobody said it, it isn't in the email.

Owners and due dates

Action items sync to Linear, Asana or Todoist with the person and the date already filled in.

Lands in the right thread

Drafts reply in the existing email thread, in Gmail or Outlook, and leave off anyone who wasn't on the call.

Ask your meetings

“What did we promise Halden in March?” gets a straight answer, with the clip attached.

30+ languages

Handles calls that switch languages halfway through, and writes the follow-up in the language of the thread.

Send rules

Let internal stand-ups go out on their own. Keep client calls waiting for your nod. You decide, per calendar.

Questions

The ones we hear on every demo.

Does everyone know they're being recorded?

Yes. Fieldnote joins under a visible name, posts a notice in the meeting chat and leaves if anyone types “stop”. In person, your phone shows a recording banner the whole time.

Can I record without a bot joining?

Yes. The desktop app can capture call audio directly, and the iPhone app records the room. Attendees still get the disclosure email.

Will it send anything without asking me?

Not unless you set a rule. By default every draft waits for you. Most teams auto-send internal stand-ups and keep client calls on manual.

Which email does it work with?

Gmail and Outlook, shared inboxes included. Drafts show up in your normal Drafts folder, threaded with the right conversation.
