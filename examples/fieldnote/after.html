Four boxes. We tick three.

  1. Recorded

    Add it to the call, or put your phone on the table. It says out loud that it's recording, so nobody has to ask.

  2. Sorted

    Decisions, dates and who said they'd do what get pulled out of the talk. "Friday. No, Monday." goes in as Monday.

  3. Drafted

    The email is in your drafts when the meeting ends, addressed to the people who were in it, written the way you usually write.

  4. Sent

    This box is yours. Fieldnote never sends anything on its own. Go on, tick it.There. That was the whole job.

Questions

  • Re: Does everyone know it's recording?

    Yes. It says so when it starts and shows it for as long as it's running. If someone asks you to stop it, it's one button.

  • Re: What if it gets something wrong?

    It's a draft, and nothing goes out until you send it. Every line links to the moment it came from, so checking takes a click, not a re-listen.

  • Re: Will it sound like a robot?

    It writes the way your last few emails read. If you never open with "I hope this finds you well", neither will it.
