Nothing is sent until you press Send.

Fieldnote writes into your drafts folder and stops there. It has no way to email anyone on its own.

  • It says it’s recording.

    It joins under its own name and posts a notice in the chat. Anyone on the call can remove it.

  • You choose when it’s deleted.

    Keep them for a year, a week, or delete the audio as soon as the email goes out.

  • Only the people who were there.

    Transcripts are shared with the attendees you choose, never with your whole workspace by default.

  • Your calls don’t train models.

    Recordings and transcripts are used to write your drafts and for nothing else.
