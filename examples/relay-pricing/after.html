A segment is 160 characters
until it isn't.

Carriers charge for each part of a text. Stay inside GSM-7, the alphabet SMS was built on, and a part holds 160 characters. One character from outside it switches the whole text to Unicode, and a part holds 70.

The usual cause is a curly quote or a long dash pasted from a doc. Emoji do it too. Inside GSM-7, nine characters such as [ and € count as two.

Ask before you send.

Add dry_run to any request. Relay returns the encoding, the segment count and the price, and sends nothing. Put it in your tests and a template that grows a segment fails CI before it reaches an invoice.

Dry runs are free on every plan. This one is reading the message on the screen up top.

Questions about the bill.

    

      
Do I pay for texts that fail?
You pay for segments a carrier accepts. If we can't hand a segment over, it isn't billed.

      
What if I go past my included segments?
Live and Volume keep sending at the rate in the table, and the extra shows on that month's invoice. Sandbox pauses until next month.

      
Are carrier fees extra?
No. US and Canadian rates include them.

      
Do unused segments roll over?
No. The count resets on your billing date.

      
Can I switch plans?
Whenever you like. Moving up starts right away and is prorated by the day. Moving down starts on your next billing date.

      
What about other countries?
That's on Volume. Each country has its own rate on the rate sheet, and those segments don't draw on your included ones.
