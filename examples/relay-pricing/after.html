Before your first invoice

What counts as a segment?
One SMS carries 160 GSM-7 characters. Longer messages split into segments of 153. A single character outside GSM-7 sends the whole message as UCS-2: 70 characters, or 67 per segment when split.
Will curly quotes double my bill?
No. Before counting, we swap curly quotes, long dashes and non-breaking spaces for their GSM-7 equivalents. You can turn this off per message. Emoji and letters like Ł still need UCS-2.
Do failed messages cost anything?
You pay for segments a carrier accepts. Sends we reject first, like invalid numbers or recipients who replied STOP, are free.
How does test mode work?
Test keys deliver real texts to up to five verified numbers at no charge, with no card on file. Switch to a live key when you are ready.
Can I change plans mid-month?
Yes. Upgrades apply at once and the platform fee is prorated. Downgrades take effect on the 1st.
Is there anything to roll over?
No bundles, so nothing expires. You pay for what you send, plus the platform fee on Growth.
