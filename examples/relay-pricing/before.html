Contact billing

What counts as a segment?

An SMS is billed in segments of 160 characters, or 70 if it contains emoji or non-Latin characters. Longer messages are split and each part is billed. Every API response includes the segment count.

Am I charged for failed messages?

No. If a carrier rejects a message or it can't be delivered, it isn't billed. You still get the failure webhook with the carrier's reason code.

What happens if I go over 10,000 on Growth?

Extra segments are billed at $0.0071 at the end of the month. Nothing is throttled and your plan never changes without you.

Can I test without paying?

Yes. Test mode is free on every plan and returns realistic delivery webhooks without touching a carrier. New accounts also get $5 of live credit.

How are international rates set?

Per destination country, based on carrier cost plus a fixed margin. The full table is in the dashboard and published as JSON, so you can price messages before sending.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
